World Laureates Summit

A diagnosis of our time — and a prescription

Science is entering a golden age — yet the institutions built for an earlier era can no longer carry it. WLA names the challenge, and gives OPENSCI as the answer.

The challenge · past institutions

Why the old order can no longer carry science

  • Short-term metrics squeeze out long-term basic research.
  • Closed systems keep knowledge and process out of view.
  • AI makes the attribution of contribution far harder.
  • Public trust in science is eroding.
The solution · OPENSCI

OPENSCI — the answer for a golden age

WLA embeds OPENSCI as the institutional response: contribution in the AI era must remain visible, attributable, auditable and returnable.

01

Visible

The research process, made openly legible.

02

Attributable

Contribution weighted between scientists and algorithms.

03

Auditable

Peer review and data flow traceable end to end.

04

Returnable

Findings flow back into the shared knowledge base.

OPENSCI is a protocol for scientific contribution — not a financial system.
